Wood-Fired Sourdough, Baked Daily in West Asheville

Sable & Rye Bakehouse has been baking naturally leavened sourdough, laminated pastries, and made-to-order celebration cakes out of our West Asheville storefront since 2019. Every loaf goes through a 36-hour cold ferment and bakes in our wood-fired oven — no commercial yeast, ever.

Walk in Tuesday through Sunday for the daily bread case, or order ahead for pickup. We also supply wholesale sourdough to a handful of Asheville restaurants and coffee shops — see our menu & ordering page for the full lineup and lead times.
