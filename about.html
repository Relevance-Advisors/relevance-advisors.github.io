About Sable & Rye

Sable & Rye Bakehouse opened in 2019 in a converted auto-body shop on Haywood Rd. Owner and head baker Marguerite Sable trained in Lyon before returning to Asheville to build a bakery around a single starter culture — "the mother" — that's been fed daily since opening week.

Everything in the case is naturally leavened: no commercial yeast, no dough conditioners, and a 36-hour cold ferment for the sourdough line. We mill a portion of our own flour from North Carolina grain in-house.

We're a crew of eight, most of whom started as regulars before they started as bakers.
