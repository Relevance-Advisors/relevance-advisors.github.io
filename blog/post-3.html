Gluten-Free? Dairy-Free? What We Can (and Can't) Do for Dietary Needs

By Marguerite Sable · July 28, 2026

We get some version of this question almost every day at the counter, so here's the honest, specific answer instead of a vague "ask staff" sign.

Gluten

Nothing we sell is gluten-free, and we can't make that claim for anything baked here — our kitchen mills and handles wheat flour constantly, so even a naturally leavened sourdough isn't a safe bet for celiac disease or a severe gluten intolerance. Some customers with mild sensitivity find our long-fermented sourdough easier to digest than commercial bread, which we cover in more detail in our post on the 36-hour ferment — but that's a digestibility note, not a gluten-free claim.

Dairy and eggs

ItemContains dairy/egg
Country Sourdough, Rye & Caraway, Multigrain, BaguetteNo
Almond Croissant, Kouign-amann, Morning BunYes — butter, most contain egg wash
Custom cakesVaries by recipe — ask when ordering

Nuts

The almond croissant is made in the same kitchen as our nut-free bread line, and we don't have a dedicated nut-free prep area. If a tree nut allergy is severe, we'd rather you know that up front than find out from a shared surface.

Vegan

All four of our daily bread loaves are vegan as written — no dairy, eggs, or animal-derived dough conditioners. Pastries are not.
