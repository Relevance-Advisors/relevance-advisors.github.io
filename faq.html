Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. We keep a daily bread case and pastry counter stocked for walk-in customers Tuesday through Sunday, 7am–3pm. Popular items like the country sourdough sell out by early afternoon most days, so come early if you want a specific loaf.

Do you have gluten-free or dairy-free options?

We don't currently bake a certified gluten-free product — our kitchen handles wheat flour daily, so we can't guarantee a gluten-free environment. Most of our bread line is dairy-free, but pastries generally contain butter and eggs. See our journal post on dietary needs for a full breakdown by item.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call or email at least a day ahead for bread and pastry pickup, and give us two weeks' notice for custom cakes. Same-day walk-in stock is first come, first served.

What are your wholesale minimums?

Wholesale accounts start at 20 par-baked loaves per delivery, with standing weekly or twice-weekly delivery days depending on route. Reach out through the contact page with your restaurant or cafe's details to set up an account.

Is there parking?

There's street parking on Haywood Rd, plus a free overflow lot behind the building for the West Asheville storefront.

Do you offer baking classes?

Yes — we run a monthly Sourdough 101 class covering levain building, shaping, and cold-ferment timing. See the catering & classes page for the next scheduled date and how to reserve a spot.
