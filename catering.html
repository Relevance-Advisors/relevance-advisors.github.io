Catering & Baking Classes

Event catering

We put together bread and pastry spreads for offices, weddings, and private events — think a grazing board of sliced sourdough varieties, a case of morning pastries for an early meeting, or a full dessert table built around one of our custom cakes. Packages are quoted per head with a two-week lead time; see the menu & ordering page for the base items we build from, then reach out via the contact page with your headcount and date.

Sourdough 101: Baking Class

Once a month, Marguerite runs a three-hour hands-on class covering how to build and maintain a levain, shape a boule and a batard, and read a cold ferment by feel instead of the clock — the same process behind our 36-hour cold-ferment loaves. Class size is capped at ten. Next session: Saturday, September 12, 2026, 10am, $65 per seat, starter culture and take-home loaf included.

Email or call to reserve a spot — seats are first come, first served and usually fill about two weeks out.
